About My Journey

Creating with patience, color, and emotion

I am Chitra Valpati, the artist behind Cutistic Art. My work explores expressive visual storytelling through digital artwork, watercolors, mandala patterns, pencil shading, and acrylic color studies.

Each artwork reflects a different part of my practice: calm seascapes, spiritual silhouettes, intricate black-and-white detail, expressive portraits, and bright floral compositions.

I am excited to apply for the Junior Artist role at The Yellow Brush studio, where I hope to keep learning, contribute fresh ideas, and grow inside a creative team.
